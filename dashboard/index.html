Alcovia Ops Dashboards

Two views the founder's office can run the week from: how the funnel and revenue are tracking, and whether each Alcovian is getting what was promised and making progress. Pick a timeline on either page; every number, chart and flag recomputes for that period with a delta against the period before.

About the data

All numbers here are dummy data: about 30k rows generated by a seeded random generator, so every reload shows the same values. Built-in storylines: one fatiguing creative, one slow landing page, overdue invoices, upcoming trial renewals and three students who are sliding. In production the same tables would sit in Supabase (Postgres), filled by n8n from ClickUp, Razorpay, Meta Ads, the Alcovia app and wearables, so an advisor only has to fill in short ClickUp forms.
